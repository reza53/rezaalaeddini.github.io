Jeremy Lovato is a graduate student in the Chemistry program at Youngstown State University, where he specializes in forensic chemistry and the detection of drug metabolites in complex biological samples like latent fingerprints using Raman spectroscopy. He holds a 2023 Bachelor of Science in Applied Science in Forensic Biology with a Chemistry minor from the same institution and possesses a strong analytical laboratory background featuring hands-on experience with LC-MS/MS, ELISA, and FTIR, alongside a firm grounding in regulated SOP compliance. His professional foundation includes a formative internship with the Mahoning County Coroner’s Office—where he assisted with autopsies, evidence documentation, and toxicological specimen collection—as well as professional affiliations with the American Academy of Forensic Sciences and the American Chemical Society. Ultimately, after earning an award-winning master's degree, Jeremy plans to pursue a Ph.D. in Forensic Sciences to dedicate his career to advancing trace analysis, forensic toxicology, and analytical methods that improve the interpretation of forensic evidence.
