Witness testimony, while often compelling, it can be highly unreliable due to memory decay, stress, and suggestive questioning. Cross-examination is used to test credibility and expose inconsistencies.

    Federal eyewitness reliability is governed by the Supreme Court's two-step Manson v. Brathwaite test, which excludes identifications only if they resulted from unnecessarily suggestive police procedures and are fundamentally unreliable.
</div>

FRE Rule 702 (Expert Testimony): Defense attorneys frequently try to introduce cognitive psychologists as experts to educate juries on why human memory fails (e.g., cross-racial identification issues or weapon focus). Federal judges have wide discretion under Rule 702 to permit or deny this testimony based on whether it will truly assist the jury.
